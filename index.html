About Us

Welcome to Sail Clermont Lakes! We are dedicated to providing the best sailing experiences on the beautiful lakes of Clermont. Our team of experienced instructors is passionate about sharing their love for sailing with you.

Enjoy our beautiful, natural surroundings and local wildlife, as well as views of downtown Clermont and the Citrus Tower.

The Vessel

Our vessel is a 1989 Catalina 25 MKII. She features a wing keel shoal draft and a hull speed of 6 knots powered by a brand new 2026 Honda 9.9 Longshaft 4-stoke Powerthrust outboard. She is hull number 889 out of 900 which makes her the 11th to last ever made from the total production. She was made for coastal cruising but has and always will be a lake boat. Whether we are catching the afternoon breeze for a lesson or enjoying a steady sunset charter, our boat is kept to the highest standards of safety and maintenance.

Perfect for small groups, couples and individual instruction, it offers a stable and responsive ride, making it the ideal platform for beginners to learn the ropes and for experienced sailors to enjoy a refined day on the water.
